Family Finance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are

Family Finance is a household finance app for tracking expenses, salaries, loans, investments, insurance and bank balances with the people you choose to share a workspace with. It is run by an individual developer. Contact: a.harikrishna108@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

DataWhy we need it
Your name and email addressTo create your account, sign you in, and show you (and people you invite) who is in a workspace.
Your passwordTo sign you in. It is stored only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it.
The finance information you type in: expenses, salaries, names of family members, loans, investments, insurance policies (including policy numbers if you enter them), bank names and balances, notesThis is the app's purpose. We store it so you can see it again and share it with members of your workspace.
Workspace membership and roles, and a log of archive/restore actionsTo decide who can view, edit or delete what, and to show who did what.
Sign-in sessions (refresh tokens)To keep you signed in. On your phone the session is kept in the device's secure storage.
Technical request logs (IP address, time, requested address)To keep the service secure and working, for example to limit abuse. They are rotated and kept only briefly.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or advertising identifiers. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Who can see your data

  • People in your workspace. Everything inside a workspace is visible to its members, according to their role (viewer, member or admin). You decide who is invited.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or give it to third parties, except where the law requires it.
  • The developer can technically access the database for maintenance, and will not look at your content except to fix a problem you report or as required by law.

Where and how it is stored

  • Data is stored on servers in Mumbai, India (Amazon Web Services), and is sent between the app and the server over encrypted HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed. Access to each workspace is checked on the server for every request.
  • Database backups are taken every night and kept for 14 days, then deleted.

How long we keep it, and deleting your data

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • You can delete your account yourself in the app: More → Delete account. See how account deletion works, including what happens to shared workspaces.
  • When you delete your account, your personal data is removed from the live database straight away. Copies in nightly backups disappear when those backups expire, within 14 days.

Your choices

  • You can view and change your finance entries in the app at any time.
  • To correct your name or email, or to ask what we hold about you, write to a.harikrishna108@gmail.com.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or by email if you cannot open the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and its date, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: a.harikrishna108@gmail.com.

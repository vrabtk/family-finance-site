Delete your Family Finance account

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open the Family Finance app and sign in.
  2. Go to More (bottom right).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete my account.

The deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone. You are signed out of the app.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, optional mobile number and password hash.
  • Your sign-in sessions on every device.
  • Every workspace that only you belong to, with all the expenses, salaries, loans, investments, insurance, bank entries and notes in it.
  • Your membership of any workspaces shared with other people.
  • Your entries in the archive log.

What stays, and why

  • Shared workspaces you own. If other people use a workspace you own, ownership passes to its longest-standing other admin, and the data stays for them. The entries you made there stay too, but no longer show your name.
  • If there is no other admin in a workspace you own, the app asks you to make someone an admin first (or remove the other members), so a shared workspace is never left without an owner.
  • A one-way code of your email address. So the same address cannot sign up again, we keep a keyed hash of it. It cannot be turned back into your address. Ask us if you need it lifted.
  • Backups. Nightly backups made before you deleted may still contain your data. They are deleted automatically within 14 days.

Cannot open the app?

Email a.harikrishna108@gmail.com from the address registered on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel anything else: the app is free and there is no subscription to cancel.
